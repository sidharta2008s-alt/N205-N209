Windmill blades: smooth vs. tubercles

Tubercles are the bumps on a humpback whale’s flipper. On a turbine blade they break the airflow into small streamwise vortices that keep it attached longer. Raise the wind speed and watch the smooth blade stall first.

Power vs. wind speed

Simplified stall-regulated model, 40 m blades: both rotors share the same size and wind. The tubercle rotor gives up about 2% peak efficiency but stalls later and more gradually, so it keeps producing power in stronger wind. Behind the rotor, smooth blades shed a chaotic wake while tubercle blades leave a calmer one. Numbers are illustrative, not measured.
